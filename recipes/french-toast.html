French Toast

Description

French toast is a dish of sliced bread soaked in beaten eggs and often milk or cream, then pan-fried.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Cinnamon
  • Vanilla extract
  • Butter
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
  2. Add milk, sugar, cinnamon, vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt.
  3. Whisk everything together until well combined.
  4. Heat a pan over medium heat and add some butter.
  5. Dip a slice of bread into the egg mixture, coating both sides. Don't soak it for too long unless the bread is thick.
  6. Place the bread in the hot pan.
  7. Cook until the bottom is golden brown, about 2-3 minutes.
  8. Flip and cook the other side until golden brown.
  9. Repeat with the remaining bread, adding more butter when needed.
  10. Serve hot with honey, maple syrup, powdered sugar, fruit, or whatever you like.
