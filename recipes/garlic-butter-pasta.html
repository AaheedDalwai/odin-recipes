Garlic Butter Pasta

Description

Garlic butter pasta is a simple and flavorful pasta dish made by tossing cooked pasta in a sauce of butter, garlic, black pepper, and chilli flakes. It takes only about 10-15 minutes to prepare and requires very few ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Black pepper
  • Salt
  • Chilli flakes

Steps

  1. Boil water in a pot and add salt.
  2. Add the pasta and cook until tender.
  3. Save a little pasta water, then drain the pasta.
  4. Melt butter in a pan over low-medium heat.
  5. Add chopped garlic and cook for about 30-60 seconds.
  6. Add chilli flakes and black pepper.
  7. Add the cooked pasta.
  8. Add a small splash of the reserved pasta water.
  9. Toss everything together for 1-2 minutes.
